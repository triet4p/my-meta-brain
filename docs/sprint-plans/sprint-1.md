## Downstream Contracts

Key vault do user bảo quản, chỉ application giữ trong memory khi unlocked; connectors không giữ key hoặc đọc vault. Grant authoritative chốt resource IDs/revisions, operations, expiry, egress consent/generation. Token opaque random chỉ redeem một lần lấy scoped session, không decryption key; no OS-bound identity/peer isolation claim.

Catalog là projection của metadata owner công bố, không private index. Group/zone selection dùng preview rồi materialize scope; item/revision/membership mới không tự mở quyền. Raw-source expansion riêng, không arbitrary path. Owner unlock/approve không suy từ body, tên agent hoặc same-owner OS token.

S1 dùng synthetic encrypted resource fixtures với crypto/storage contract tái dùng ở S2, không tạo canonical truth store thứ hai. S2 thêm domain/revision/index thật; S4 mở rộng tools; S5 UI dùng cùng owner APIs. Startup/restart locked; lock/revoke chặn private reads/jobs/cache, invalidates pending tokens/live sessions; không hồi sinh used token sau crash.

### Runtime, key boundary, IPC and packaging decisions

- **Language/UI:** C#/.NET 10 LTS, WPF, one core/application/connections policy; retained D007, không thêm stack mới vì thay security model.
- **Application lifecycle:** non-admin local application/service; startup locked, owner unlock và key chỉ trong application memory. SCM virtual-identity provisioning không là prerequisite của vault security; packaging lifecycle thực được kiểm chứng S8-T1.
- **IPC:** local versioned named pipes giữ transport; agent authority chuyển từ AppContainer bindings sang token-redeemed server-side grant. Owner approval/key management tách agent surface, không suy user intent từ same-owner SID.
- **Packaging:** self-contained win-x64; không staging/rebuilding/patching Codex hoặc OMP runtime để đáp ứng AppContainer. Không thay owner installed profiles/auth/session.
- **Crypto:** standard authenticated encryption, versioned key/recovery/format và private persistence; specific package/KDF/index protection chọn/record ở S1-T2/S2-T7 bằng evidence, không tự tạo crypto.
- **MCP:** historical actual OMP/Codex resource calls là compatibility references; CR01 token connector path vẫn phải chạy trên official clients ở T9/S4-T1. ACP hoặc helper calls không thay R24.

### Consent-safe dataset and frozen targets

S1-T1 executable probes used only synthetic literals (`fixture-agent`, `fixture-owner` and a Rust package banner); no corpus generator or index was run.

The planned nonprivate benchmark fixture is deterministic synthetic Vietnamese/English content with code symbols, decision/question/research/intention shapes, revisions, stale items and conflicts. It was not generated or indexed during S1-T1 and does not evidence the owner's corpus distribution.

Synthetic stress-fixture sizes are 1,000 records (smoke), 10,000 (baseline) and 100,000 (growth), with 4 KiB mean body size and p95 at most 16 KiB. A fixed 200-query synthetic bilingual set with known result IDs is planned only; neither fixture nor query set was generated or indexed, and neither represents the owner corpus.

The owner-authorized bounded read-only sample contains six existing source documents: two from Meta Brain, three from ecommerce-agent-databricks and one Bridge Research kickoff note, totaling 79,483 bytes. The fixed manifest, UTC timestamps, observed decision-entry count, content shape and practical query plan are recorded in the redacted S1-T1 artifact. This is a purposive source-document sample, not a statistically representative distribution or a 500 normalized-memory-record holdout; no 200 owner-approved recall prompts or relevance labels exist. Never scan `.codex`/`.omp` profiles or copy session transcripts by default. No private session transcript was read.

| Metric | Frozen target before tuning |
| --- | --- |
| **Local recall latency** | p95 at most 250 ms on 1,000 records, 500 ms on 10,000, and 1,000 ms on 100,000; excludes any external provider call. |
| **Owner UI latency** | p95 at most 200 ms for local navigation/control actions and 1,500 ms from search submit to visible results on 100,000 records, including service IPC. |
| **Indexing** | At least 100 records/second on a cold 10,000-record fixture; a cold 100,000-record rebuild completes within 20 minutes. |
| **Idle CPU** | Service plus idle owner client average at most 1% of total host CPU over a 5-minute post-warm-up sample with no jobs or polling. |
| **Idle RAM** | Service working set at most 256 MiB; service plus open-but-idle owner client at most 512 MiB. |
| **Disk** | For 100,000 records at 4 KiB mean body, persistent product data (canonical, metadata, index, WAL and cache; excluding source sessions) at most 1.5 GiB and derived data at most 2× canonical body bytes. Self-contained installed binaries at most 1 GiB; report both separately. |
| **Provider spend** | $0 by default. Only after explicit owner opt-in: at most $0.25 per job and $5 per rolling 30 days, with at most 10,000 input and 2,000 output tokens per job. Unknown/unpriced model costs deny the call; no provider was called in S1-T1. |

Measure p95 from at least 1,000 timed local queries per size after 100 warm-ups; report cold-index rebuild, a 5-minute idle sample and on-disk directory sizes separately. S8-T5 reports observed values beside these targets; it must not relax them to pass. These are budget decisions based on the measured 4-core/8-logical, 16-GiB Windows host and a bounded 100,000-record growth case, not achieved performance. The current free-memory snapshot was only about 1.86 GiB, so it is not an idle benchmark baseline.
